# Technology leadership, architecture and software delivery.

Purple Leaves provides independent technical consultancy, helping organisations make sound technology decisions, develop software and deliver projects from concept to production.

[Get in touch](#get-in-touch)

---

## What we do

### 01 — Technology leadership

Fractional CTO support, technical strategy, architecture decisions and engineering oversight for organisations that need experienced technical direction without a full-time appointment.

### 02 — Software and cloud engineering

Software architecture, application development, systems integration, cloud infrastructure and modernisation, combining architectural thinking with hands-on engineering.

### 03 — End-to-end project delivery

Taking defined software projects from requirements and design through development, deployment and handover. Specialist collaborators can be involved where the scope requires it.

---

## How we work

Good technology decisions begin with understanding the problem.

The emphasis is on clear requirements, appropriate architecture and maintainable systems. The aim is to deliver practical solutions that fit the organisation, rather than introduce complexity for its own sake.

- Understand the problem
- Choose appropriate technology
- Build for maintainability
